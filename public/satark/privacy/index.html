Satark — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Who we are

Samsara Smartworld Private Limited (“Samsara”, “we”), Flat No. 101-102, Mercantile House, 15 K G Marg, New Delhi 110001, India, publishes the Satark app on Google Play. This policy covers the app and the servers we operate for it, for which we are the Data Fiduciary. Organisations that run Satark on their own servers look after the data held there under their own privacy notice.

2. Who can use Satark

We issue accounts to authorised users aged 18 or over.

3. Information we collect

  • Account: username, name, role and district, sign-in credentials and session token.
  • Location: precise location about every 30 seconds while you are on duty, shown by a persistent notification, and once at sign-in if you have granted location access.
  • Photos, video, audio and files: what you capture or attach, and your camera and microphone stream while you broadcast live or join a video meeting. Video meetings are recorded, and the recordings are stored on our servers.
  • Video analytics: if you turn on video analytics for a live session, the video is analysed for faces, vehicle number plates, crowds and objects. Face matching processes biometric data. Video analytics is off unless you choose it for that session.
  • Device: Android ID, an app installation ID, a device fingerprint (a hash of the Android ID and hardware details), device model, OS version, battery and network status, and push token.
  • Usage: alerts you view or acknowledge, actions you take, error logs and IP address.
  • Automatic crash diagnostics: Google Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports and technical diagnostics in production releases, including crash stack traces, error categories, HTTP status codes, device model, OS/app version, crash timestamps, app state, network connection type, installation identifiers and session-start timing used for stability metrics. Reports may be stored on your device and uploaded when the app restarts and a network connection is available.

We collect only the information listed above. Apart from the single location reading at sign-in, location is collected only while you are on duty.

4. How we use it

To sign you in and keep your session secure, provide the app's features, deliver notifications, diagnose crashes, improve app stability and prevent misuse. We use it only for these purposes. Consent requirements depend on the information collected and applicable privacy rules; where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it by contacting us using the details in section 9. Crash reports are sent automatically so we can keep the app working, and cannot be turned off in the app. Your information stays with Samsara and the service providers below; it is never sold or used for advertising.

5. Who helps us provide the service

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our servers.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications (push token and notification text).
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics receives automatic crash and error diagnostics to help us find and fix faults and measure app stability. We filter app-submitted error details to reduce disclosure of officer details, locations, tokens and sensitive URLs, and do not set an account identifier in Crashlytics. This filtering does not cover every native SDK-generated report. SDK diagnostics and installation identifiers mean complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed.
  • Google connects live audio and video calls (your IP address).
  • MapTiler displays maps (your IP address and the map area you view).
  • Google Maps shows directions when you choose to open a location there.

Video meetings and video analytics, including face matching and number-plate recognition, run on Samsara's own service. Each service provider works under a written agreement that requires it to protect your information and to delete it when we ask. We share information with authorities when Indian law, a court or a competent authority requires it.

6. Where it is stored

Our servers and backups are hosted by Amazon Web Services. Our service providers may carry out some processing, such as notifications, crash diagnostics and maps, outside India.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account records, location, media, reports and server logs for 1 year from their creation or last use, then erase them, together with their backups, and instruct our service providers to delete any copies they hold. When your access ends or you ask us to erase your data, it leaves active use and is held securely, for security and legal compliance only, until that period ends, as Indian law requires.

Google Firebase Crashlytics has a separate retention schedule: crash traces and associated identifiers are kept for 90 days before Google starts removing them from live and backup systems. See Firebase privacy information.

8. How we protect it

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Credentials are kept in Android's encrypted storage and passwords as hashes on our servers. Access is limited by role, screenshots and screen recording are blocked, and app data is kept out of device cloud backups. If a security incident affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as Indian law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw your consent, or nominate someone to act for you. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete my account in the app, or email info@samsara-smartworld.com with the subject “Delete my Satark account”. We respond within 30 days. You can manage location, camera, microphone and notification permissions in Android Settings at any time, and going off duty stops location sharing.

For any concern, contact our Grievance Officer, Abhinav Jha, at info@samsara-smartworld.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once its complaints process applies; the law asks you to use our grievance process first (section 13(3) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

Crash reporting runs automatically in production releases. Satark does not offer an in-app crash-report sharing switch. Android does not request a separate runtime permission for crash reporting; this does not remove applicable privacy notice or consent requirements. For questions or requests about diagnostic data, including withdrawal where processing relies on consent, contact us using the details above. Previously uploaded reports remain subject to the retention schedule in section 7.

10. Updates to this policy

We post updates here with a new date, and tell you in the app before any significant change applies.
