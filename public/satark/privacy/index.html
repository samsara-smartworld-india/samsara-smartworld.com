Satark — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

Samsara Smartworld Private Limited (“Samsara”, “we”), Flat No. 101-102, Mercantile House, 15 K G Marg, New Delhi 110001, India, publishes the Satark app on Google Play. This policy covers the app and the servers we operate for it, for which we are the Data Fiduciary. Organisations that run Satark on their own servers look after the data held there under their own privacy notice.

2. Who can use Satark

We issue accounts to authorised users aged 18 or over.

3. Information we collect

  • Account: username, name, role and district, sign-in credentials and session token.
  • Location: precise location about every 30 seconds while you are on duty, shown by a persistent notification, and once at sign-in if you have granted location access.
  • Photos, video, audio and files: what you capture or attach, and your camera and microphone stream while you broadcast live or join a video meeting.
  • Device: Android ID, an app installation ID, a device fingerprint (a hash of the Android ID and hardware details), device model, OS version, battery and network status, and push token.
  • Usage: alerts you view or acknowledge, actions you take, error logs and IP address.

We collect only the information listed above, and location only while you are on duty.

4. How we use it

To sign you in and keep your session secure, provide the app's features, deliver notifications, fix faults and prevent misuse. We use it only for these purposes, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Your information stays with Samsara and the service providers below; it is never sold or used for advertising.

5. Who helps us provide the service

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our servers.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications (push token and notification text).
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error reports (device model, OS version, app version and technical details of the fault) so we can fix problems.
  • Google connects live audio and video calls (your IP address).
  • MapTiler displays maps (your IP address and the map area you view).
  • Google Maps shows directions when you choose to open a location there.

Video meetings run on Samsara's own service. We share information with authorities when Indian law, a court or a competent authority requires it.

6. Where it is stored

Our servers and backups are hosted by Amazon Web Services. Our service providers may carry out some processing, such as face matching, number-plate recognition, notifications and maps, outside India.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account records, location, media, reports and logs for 1 year from their creation or last use, then erase them, together with their backups. When your access ends or you ask us to erase your data, it leaves active use and is held securely, for security and legal compliance only, until that period ends, as Indian law requires.

8. How we protect it

Credentials are kept in Android's encrypted storage and passwords as hashes on our servers. Access is limited by role, screenshots and screen recording are blocked, and app data is kept out of device cloud backups. If a security incident affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as Indian law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw your consent, or nominate someone to act for you. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete my account in the app, or email info@samsara-smartworld.com with the subject “Delete my Satark account”. We respond within 30 days. You can manage location, camera, microphone and notification permissions in Android Settings at any time, and going off duty stops location sharing.

For any concern, contact our Grievance Officer, Abhinav Jha, at info@samsara-smartworld.com or +91 80765 23416. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Updates to this policy

We post updates here with a new date, and tell you in the app before any significant change applies.
